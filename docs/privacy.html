Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Nail Play is a casual puzzle game made by Tutty Lab. We built it to work entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. Nail Play does not collect, store on our servers, share, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars and settings) is saved only on your device. It never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Network access

The game runs offline. It does not send requests to our servers or to third-party services.

Children

Because we collect no data, Nail Play collects no information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/Tutty-Lab/nail-game/issues.

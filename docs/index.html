Nail Play Support

Nail Play is a cozy collection of nail-salon puzzle games: Polish Painter, Gradient Mixer, Polish Sort, Nail Match, Merge, Pop, Memory and Catch — each with its own levels.

Get help

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/Tutty-Lab/nail-game/issues and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

FAQ

  • Where is my progress saved? Only on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.
  • Do I need an account or internet? No. The game works offline with no sign-in.
  • Are there ads or purchases? No, none at all.

Privacy

Nail Play collects no data. Read the Privacy Policy.
